# LuxDot v4.3.120 — Research Multi-View

- Added SKY / UNIVERSE / CIRCUIT synchronized Research Atlas views.
- UNIVERSE adds galaxies, cluster anchors, systems, planets, moons and asteroids.
- CIRCUIT adds PCB-style family zones, LuxDot / CPU, research chips, transistors and orthogonal traces.
- SIGNAL PULSE illuminates direct relation paths from the selected node and dims unrelated graph elements.
- Switching views preserves the graph search, family/topic filters, language context and selected node.
- The last selected view is remembered locally.
- Reduced-motion accessibility is supported.
- Implemented as a reversible visual layer over the existing research dataset and renderer.
