# LuxDot WITNESS — Privacy & Editorial Protocol v1

1. Storage consent and publication consent are separate.
2. Default is preserve-only; publication is never inferred from submission.
3. Minimize identity data. Name, email and phone are optional.
4. Do not publish exact live locations of vulnerable people.
5. Child-related testimony requires a dedicated safeguarding workflow before public release.
6. Original evidence should be preserved unchanged; derivatives must be labeled.
7. Every testimony gets a Witness ID; evidence gets SHA-256 fingerprints.
8. Verification states are editorial states, not judicial findings.
9. Public records must be redacted according to the contributor's chosen privacy level.
10. Contributors need a future mechanism to request access, correction, withdrawal, or deletion where legally applicable.
11. Sensitive/special-category data must not be centrally collected until a reviewed backend, access controls, retention schedule, encryption, incident response, and GDPR legal basis are in place.
12. Never promise absolute confidentiality or absolute security.
