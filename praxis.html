Evidence protocol. “Traditional” means the practice is documented as a tradition; it does not establish its supernatural claims. “Practical” marks operational methods with observable outcomes. “Mixed” marks areas where evidence depends on the specific outcome studied. “Unverified” marks claims for which LuxDot should not present a scientific mechanism as established fact.
