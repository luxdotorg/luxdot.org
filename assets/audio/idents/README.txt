LuxDot hourly station IDs — v4.9.0

Sequence at the top of the hour:
1) hour-strike.wav
2) station-id-<lang>.wav
3) luxdot-signature.wav
4) return to live program and re-sync to current broadcast time.

The station-ID voice files are provisional locally-generated TTS masters.
They can be replaced later by natural human recordings WITHOUT changing code,
as long as the filenames remain the same.
Arabic is intentionally scheduled at 00:00, 12:00 and 23:00.
Timezone: Europe/Amsterdam.
