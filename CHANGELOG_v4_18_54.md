# LuxDot v4.18.54 — Chaam Midwives Living Memory

- Adds two linked Living Memory dossiers for Tetje de Vries and Barbiers in Chaam.
- Uses original LuxDot field photographs of both graves and the local Heemkundekring Ledevaert thank-you plaques; no generated artwork is used.
- Connects the two dossiers to one another and registers both as Living Memory nodes.
- Preserves directly readable Dutch inscriptions and clearly marks uncertain, faint grave details rather than guessing them.
- Records the local plaque testimony that the two midwives served Chaam mothers, travelled at night when needed, and helped approximately 2,000 children come into the world during the documented period.
