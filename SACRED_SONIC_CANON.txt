Production rule:
- Human voice before music.
- No instrumental bed beneath sacred recitation.
- Silence separates traditions.
- No recording is called authentic liturgical/cantillation audio unless provenance and reuse rights are verified.

Why not bundle any recording found online?
Mechon-Mamre's Hebrew Bible audio explicitly says it is used with permission from Talking Bibles International and directs others to request permission. LuxDot therefore does not redistribute it without authorization.
Wikimedia Commons contains openly licensed Sanskrit recordings, but the exact selected Gita 16:24 recording was not verified in the available set, so LuxDot does not mislabel a substitute.
