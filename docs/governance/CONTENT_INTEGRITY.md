# Research & Content Integrity

For research-facing additions, use four distinct evidence states where applicable:

1. **Documented fact** — supported by an independently verifiable source.
2. **Tradition** — religious, cultural, folkloric or oral tradition, labeled as such.
3. **Symbolic interpretation** — a stated resemblance or interpretive reading, not evidence by itself.
4. **Hypothesis/speculation** — an inference or proposed model that remains unverified.

Dates, quotations, names and factual claims should be checked against primary or high-quality secondary sources before publication when practical.

Coincidences, numerical patterns and calendrical synchronies must not be presented as proof of supernatural causation, prophecy fulfillment or special personal status.
