# LuxDot Identity & Provenance Policy --- Draft v0.1

Open knowledge does not mean false provenance.

1.  Reusers may accurately state that material is derived from or based
    on LuxDot when that is true.
2.  Modified forks must not represent themselves as the official LuxDot
    service/publication without authorization.
3.  Attribution must not imply endorsement.
4.  Material should preserve source/provenance notices where reasonably
    possible.
5.  The LuxDot name/logo/official seals may be governed separately from
    copyright licenses to prevent confusion.
6.  This policy should not be used to suppress criticism, research,
    interoperability, truthful reference, or legitimate reuse of openly
    licensed material.
