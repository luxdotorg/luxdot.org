BOUNDARY
Ratu Adil is treated as a Javanese political-prophetic concept, not a prophet with one fixed religion. Kejawen is not represented as one standardized religion with one sacred book. Serat, suluk and related works are classified by genre and source rather than artificially canonized.

SOURCE DISCIPLINE
