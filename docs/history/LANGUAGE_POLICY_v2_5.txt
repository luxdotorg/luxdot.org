LUXDOT v2.5 LANGUAGE POLICY

CORE UI ACCESS
- Arabic (RTL)
- English (LTR)
- Dutch (LTR)
- Bahasa Indonesia (LTR)

SOURCE-LANGUAGE LAYERS
- Basa Jawa is preserved for Javanese terms, literary sources and verified source-script display
- Aksara Jawa is not used decoratively when spelling cannot be verified
- Sacred/source texts may retain original languages regardless of UI language

PRINCIPLE
A visitor should not need English to understand the primary navigation or the Nusantara/Faith architecture. Source-language material remains visible where meaningful, with context and translation.
