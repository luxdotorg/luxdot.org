Hank–Raamsdonk — Van Velthoven and war memory

A memory dossier that puts names and dates before assumed heroism. A person appearing among war victims is not automatically evidence that the person belonged to the resistance.

Established names

A

Henricus van Velthoven

Born in Hank on 28 January 1911 and died in Werkendam on 29 March 1945 from an earlier injury, according to the Brabantse Gesneuvelden register.

A

Adrianus “Arjan” van Velthoven

Born in Hank on 13 August 1913 and killed in Hank on 17 November 1944. His presence in the register is established; his exact relationship to other Van Velthoven branches still requires genealogical documents.

Limits of inference

Neither man is described as a “resistance fighter” until a resistance file, testimony, arrest record or direct local source is found. “War victim” is the currently established description.
